Getting Here

Find the School

By Rail

Oxford Railway Station is a 15-minute walk or short taxi ride from Wellington Hall. Regular services from London Paddington (55 minutes) and Birmingham New Street (1 hour).

By Coach

Oxford's Gloucester Green coach station is a 10-minute walk from campus. Oxford Tube and National Express services connect Oxford to London and major UK cities.

By Car

Stonebridge University is located within Oxford's Low Emission Zone. We recommend using Park & Ride facilities. The nearest car park is Gloucester Green (10-minute walk).
