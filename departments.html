Our Departments

Three distinguished departments at the forefront of business and financial scholarship, research, and professional development.

Department One

Finance & Banking

The Department of Finance & Banking is one of the oldest and most respected finance departments in the United Kingdom. Founded in 1902, it has produced some of the country's most distinguished financial economists, central bankers, and investment professionals.

Our research spans capital markets, asset pricing, corporate finance, and financial regulation, with close ties to the Bank of England, the FCA, and leading global financial institutions.

Department Two

Management & Strategy

The Department of Management & Strategy prepares students and researchers to understand, lead, and transform organisations of all kinds. Our faculty brings together scholars from strategy, organisational behaviour, human resource management, entrepreneurship, and innovation.

We maintain active research partnerships with FTSE 100 companies, government bodies, and international NGOs, ensuring our teaching remains grounded in real-world management challenges.

Department Three

Marketing & Commerce

The Department of Marketing & Commerce is at the intersection of theory and commercial practice. Our scholars examine consumer behaviour, brand management, digital commerce, and the analytics that underpin modern business decisions.

With dedicated laboratories for consumer research and data analytics, and partnerships with leading retail and media organisations, we offer a truly applied education in the commercial sciences.
