Research & Publications

Stonebridge faculty produce research that shapes economic policy, financial practice, and business theory at the highest international level.

Research Infrastructure

Research Centres

Finance & Banking

Centre for Financial Markets Research

Investigates the structure, efficiency, and regulation of global capital markets, with particular focus on market microstructure, derivatives pricing, and systemic risk.

Director: Prof. James Thornton · Est. 1985
Finance & Banking

Oxford Banking & Regulation Institute

Provides independent analysis of banking regulation, monetary policy, and financial stability. Advises the Bank of England, HM Treasury, and the FCA.

Director: Dr. Sarah Mallory · Est. 2003
Management & Strategy

Centre for Corporate Governance & Ethics

Examines board composition, executive accountability, stakeholder theory, and the integration of environmental and social considerations into corporate strategy.

Director: Prof. Claire Ashworth · Est. 1997
Management & Strategy

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Lab

Supports entrepreneurial research and education, running incubation programmes for student and alumni ventures and studying the conditions that enable innovation.

Director: Dr. Helena Novak · Est. 2015
Marketing & Commerce

Consumer Behaviour & Analytics Lab

Combines behavioural economics, psychology, and data science to understand how consumers make decisions and how firms can respond ethically and effectively.

Director: Prof. Isabelle Fontaine · Est. 2011
Marketing & Commerce

Digital Commerce & Trade Research Centre

Studies the evolving landscape of digital trade, platform economics, cross-border e-commerce regulation, and the future of global supply chains.

Director: Dr. David Walsh · Est. 2018
