About Admissions

How We Select Students

We seek students who demonstrate intellectual curiosity, analytical ability, and the drive to make a difference in the world of business and finance.

We consider each application holistically. Academic achievement is important, but we also value personal statement quality, contextual factors, and evidence of wider engagement with business and economics.

Home undergraduate students apply through UCAS. International and postgraduate applicants apply directly through our Admissions Portal.

Eligibility

Entry Requirements

Academic Requirements

  • Minimum 5 GCSEs at grade C/4 or above
  • Must include English Language and Mathematics
  • No A-Level requirement for Foundation entry
  • Mature applicants (21+) considered on merit

English Language (International)

  • IELTS Academic: 5.5 overall
  • No component below 5.0
  • TOEFL iBT: 72 overall
  • Pre-sessional English programme available

Academic Requirements

  • AAB–ABB at A-Level (typical offer)
  • International Baccalaureate: 34–36 points
  • GCSE Mathematics grade B/6 or above required
  • Foundation Year Distinction: guaranteed entry

English Language (International)

  • IELTS Academic: 6.5 overall
  • No component below 6.0
  • TOEFL iBT: 88 overall
  • Cambridge C1 Advanced: Grade B or above

Academic Requirements

  • Minimum 2:1 Bachelor’s degree
  • MBA requires 3+ years professional experience
  • Two references required
  • Personal statement: 600–800 words

English Language (International)

  • IELTS Academic: 7.0 overall
  • No component below 6.5
  • TOEFL iBT: 100 overall
