Academic Programmes

Our Programmes

From foundational study to advanced postgraduate research, Stonebridge offers a comprehensive suite of academic programmes in business, finance, and commerce.

Foundation Undergraduate Postgraduate
Foundation Year

Foundation in Business & Economics

Our Foundation Year is designed for students who demonstrate academic potential but require additional preparation before entering undergraduate study. It provides a thorough grounding in economics, quantitative methods, and academic English, with a direct progression route onto our BSc programmes.

Apply for Foundation
FND 101

Introduction to Business & Markets

An accessible introduction to how markets function, covering supply and demand, price mechanisms, and the role of firms and consumers in the modern economy.

📅 Full Year📍 Oxford Campus🎓 30 Credits
FND 102

Quantitative Methods for Business

Builds the mathematical and statistical foundations needed for undergraduate finance and economics — covering algebra, statistics, data interpretation and financial arithmetic.

📅 Full Year📍 Oxford Campus🎓 30 Credits
FND 103

Academic English & Communication

Develops the written and oral communication skills essential for university-level study, including essay writing, critical reading, and academic presentation.

📅 Full Year📍 Oxford Campus🎓 20 Credits
FND 104

Principles of Accounting

An introduction to double-entry bookkeeping, financial statements, and basic management accounting, preparing students for undergraduate accounting modules.

📅 Full Year📍 Oxford Campus🎓 20 Credits

Entry Requirements — Foundation

  • Minimum 5 GCSEs at grade C/4 or above, including English and Mathematics
  • IELTS 5.5 overall (no component below 5.0) for international students
  • Applicants without formal qualifications may be considered via interview and assessment
  • No prior knowledge of business or economics is required
Undergraduate Degrees

Bachelor's Programmes

Our undergraduate degrees combine rigorous academic theory with applied business practice. All programmes are three years full-time and lead to a Bachelor of Science (BSc) award. A placement year (sandwich) option is available, extending study to four years.

Apply Undergraduate
BSc · 3 Years

BSc Business & Finance

Our flagship degree covering corporate finance, business strategy, economics, and management. Graduates are highly sought by leading financial institutions and consultancies.

📅 3 Years (4 with placement)💷 £9,250/year🎓 360 Credits
BSc · 3 Years

BSc Accounting & Financial Management

A professionally-focused degree aligned with ICAEW, ACCA and CIMA accreditation pathways. Covers financial reporting, audit, taxation and management accounting.

📅 3 Years (4 with placement)💷 £9,250/year🎓 360 Credits
BSc · 3 Years

BSc Economics & Investment

Blends economic theory with applied investment analysis. Students develop quantitative skills alongside a deep understanding of macro and microeconomic policy.

📅 3 Years (4 with placement)💷 £9,250/year🎓 360 Credits
BSc · 3 Years

BSc International Business & Marketing

Examines global commerce, cross-cultural management, digital marketing, and international trade law within a comparative international framework.

📅 3 Years (4 with placement)💷 £9,250/year🎓 360 Credits
BSc · 3 Years

BSc Banking & Risk Management

Specialist preparation for careers in financial services, covering credit risk, Basel regulation, derivatives, and the structure of global banking systems.

📅 3 Years (4 with placement)💷 £9,250/year🎓 360 Credits
BSc · 3 Years

BSc Business Management & Strategy

Develops strategic thinking, leadership capability and entrepreneurial judgement, with modules in operations, HR, innovation, and corporate governance.

📅 3 Years (4 with placement)💷 £9,250/year🎓 360 Credits

Entry Requirements — Undergraduate

  • AAB–ABB at A-Level (or equivalent international qualifications)
  • Grade B/6 or above in GCSE Mathematics
  • IELTS 6.5 overall (no component below 6.0) for international students
  • Personal statement demonstrating interest in business or economics
  • Foundation Year students with distinction are guaranteed a place
Postgraduate Programmes

Master's & Postgraduate Degrees

Our postgraduate programmes are designed for graduates and professionals seeking to deepen their expertise, advance their careers, or transition into a new area of business and finance. All programmes are available full-time (12 months) or part-time (24 months).

Apply Postgraduate
MSc · 12 Months

MSc Financial Management

An intensive programme covering advanced corporate finance, asset pricing, portfolio management, and quantitative methods for financial decision-making.

📅 12 / 24 Months💷 £22,500/year🎓 180 Credits
MSc · 12 Months

MSc Banking & International Finance

Examines global banking operations, financial regulation, central bank policy, foreign exchange markets, and the architecture of international capital flows.

📅 12 / 24 Months💷 £22,500/year🎓 180 Credits
MSc · 12 Months

MSc Strategic Management

Develops senior leadership and strategic reasoning. Covers competitive strategy, innovation management, mergers & acquisitions, and corporate transformation.

📅 12 / 24 Months💷 £22,500/year🎓 180 Credits
MSc · 12 Months

MSc Marketing Analytics & Commerce

Combines marketing strategy with data analytics, covering digital commerce, consumer insights, programmatic advertising, and marketing technology.

📅 12 / 24 Months💷 £22,500/year🎓 180 Credits
MBA · 12 Months

MBA (Master of Business Administration)

Our full-time MBA is designed for professionals with three or more years of experience, combining leadership development, strategy, finance, and a major consultancy project.

📅 12 Months💷 £28,000🎓 3+ yrs experience
MRes · 12 Months

MRes Business & Finance Research

A research-focused master's degree providing advanced training in qualitative and quantitative research methods, designed as preparation for doctoral study.

📅 12 / 24 Months💷 £19,500/year🎓 180 Credits

Entry Requirements — Postgraduate

  • A minimum 2:1 Bachelor's degree (or international equivalent) in a relevant subject
  • For the MBA: minimum 3 years of relevant professional experience
  • IELTS 7.0 overall (no component below 6.5) for international students
  • Two academic or professional references
  • Personal statement of approximately 600–800 words
