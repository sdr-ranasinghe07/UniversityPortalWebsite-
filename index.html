About the School

A Legacy of Academic Distinction

Founded in 1847, the Stonebridge University School of Business & Finance stands among Europe's most respected institutions for the study of commerce, economics, and financial theory. Our collegiate tradition fosters rigorous scholarship alongside the practical acumen demanded by today's global markets.

We hold triple accreditation from AACSB, AMBA, and EQUIS — a distinction achieved by fewer than 1% of business schools worldwide — and our graduates occupy leadership positions across every major financial centre from London to Singapore.
